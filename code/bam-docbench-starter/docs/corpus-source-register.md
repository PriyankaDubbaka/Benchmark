**Access date:** 2026-09-18. Scope: BAM reference-material certificates/reports and openly licensed research papers useful for table/equation extraction. “Verified” below means the publisher/official page states the relevant license or provenance; public access alone is not treated as open licensing.

## BAM primary material

- **BAM-S005c official catalog — candidate/official source (not an open-license grant).** BAM webshop product page: <https://webshop.bam.de/webshop_en/reference-material/glass/chemical-composition/bam-s005c.html>. Evidence: product is “Multi-element glass for X-ray fluorescence analysis”; page labels it “Certified reference material: Yes,” gives disc dimensions (38 mm × 4 mm), and links both certificate and report. Product was listed at €314 when checked. Certificate: <https://webshop.bam.de/media/wysiwyg/Kategorien/Referenzmaterialien/Glaeser/Chemische_Zusammensetzung/Zertifikate/Zertifikat_BAM-S005c_rev1.pdf>. Report: <https://webshop.bam.de/media/wysiwyg/Kategorien/Referenzmaterialien/Glaeser/Chemische_Zusammensetzung/Berichte/bam_s005c_repe.pdf>.

- **BAM certificate — verified official technical source; permission unresolved.** RRR landing page: <https://rrr.bam.de/RRR/Content/EN/Downloads/RM-Certificates/RM-cert-special-materials/bam_s005c_e.html>; direct PDF: <https://rrr.bam.de/RRR/Content/EN/Downloads/RM-Certificates/RM-cert-special-materials/bam_s005c_e.pdf?__blob=publicationFile>. Evidence from certificate: certified elemental mass fractions and uncertainties, sample description, recommended XRF calibration use, participating laboratories, analytical methods, SI traceability, and statement that detailed technical report is downloadable/on request. **Version warning:** the RRR-hosted certificate examined in source research states validity until 08/2050, whereas the user-uploaded `Zertifikat_BAM-S005c_rev1.pdf` identifies itself as V2, revised 2025-03-03, and is valid until producer revocation. Treat these as distinct candidate document versions; do not substitute one for the other. The pilot is bound to the uploaded V2 file by SHA-256. This is a certificate offered by BAM, not a CC/open-license notice; do **not** assume redistribution or derivative rights.

- **BAM product provenance — verified.** BAM news: <https://rrr.bam.de/RRR/Content/EN/Standard-Articles/Reference-Materials/RM-News/rm-news-bam-s005c.html?nn=41656>. Evidence: BAM-S005c completed as a CRM for XRF; succeeds BAM-S005a/b; characterized through an ICG/DGG interlaboratory comparison; 16 laboratories; certified elements listed; intended for calibration and quality control. The separate BAM webshop “Licenses” page concerns software, not certificates: <https://webshop.bam.de/webshop_en/licenses/>. BAM Akademie terms state course materials are copyrighted and reproduction requires express written consent (<https://webshop.bam.de/akademie_en/agb/>); this is not a certificate-specific permission, but reinforces unresolved reuse. Seek written BAM permission for benchmark redistribution/derived assets. Check third-party ICG/DGG or laboratory-owned material/figures if reproducing report pages.
