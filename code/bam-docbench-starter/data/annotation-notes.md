# Annotation notes

- **Source and version.** The source is the eight-page PDF `Zertifikat_BAM-S005c_rev1.pdf`, headed **BAM-S005c Multielement Glass** and marked **BAM-S005c_V2**. Page 8 states “Accepted as BAM-CRM on 2020-08-19 (revised 2025-03-03).” The dataset document identifier is `bam-s005c-v2`; `rev1` is the uploaded filename, not the certificate revision wording.
- **Scope and counts.** `extraction_gold.json` covers only the 27 elemental rows in the summary tables: 25 certified rows on page 1 and P/Se as 2 information-only rows on page 2. Individual laboratory results (pages 3–4) and analytical-method tables (pages 5–7) are excluded from extraction records.
- **Transcription checks.** Page 1 was read as text and visually inspected as an image for row/column alignment and decimal placement. Page 2 was likewise visually inspected. The visually confirmed values include the percent rows (Al, Ca, K, Mg, Na, Si), the mg/kg rows, P=8.3 with uncertainty 2.5, and Se=2.5 with uncertainty 0.8. PDF text extraction introduces spacing artifacts (for example split digits and subscripts), so formulas were normalized to ASCII (e.g., `Al2O3`) and decimal strings were reconstructed from the rendered tables.
- **Interpretation.** Bracketed parenthetical values are calculated oxide mass fractions, not separate certified measurands. The uncertainty field is the elemental uncertainty and uses expanded uncertainty with k=2 (approximately 95% confidence). Page 2 explicitly says P and Se were not certified because inter-laboratory certification uncertainty was too large. The records retain source page for every row.
- **Retrieval annotations.** Sixteen answerable queries cover lookup, qualification, methods, provenance, usage, and cross-page comparisons, including certified-versus-information-only, elemental-versus-oxide, uncertainty, laboratory means, calibration purpose, minimum mass, traceability, revision, and method abbreviation lookup. Evidence pages and page-level relevance were assessed against the complete eight-page document; relevance is a draft page-level judgment, not a claim that other pages are semantically useless in every retrieval system.
- **Limitations.** These are machine-assisted draft reference annotations, not BAM-expert-validated gold labels. No web sources or regulatory-compliance conclusions were used. Oxide values are reported as printed/calculated in the certificate and were not independently recomputed for scoring.
